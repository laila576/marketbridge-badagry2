MARKETBRIDGE BADAGRY — WEBSITE STARTER
=======================================

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- index.html: Home page
- browse.html: Searchable request categories
- request.html: Buyer product request form
- how-it-works.html: Customer journey
- for-sellers.html: Seller landing page and interest form
- seller-form.html: Seller application form
- about.html: Brand story and principles
- trust.html: Trust and safety guidance
- contact.html: Contact form
- privacy.html: Prototype privacy notice
- styles.css: Responsive visual design
- app.js: Navigation, category search, form handling

IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH
1. "MarketBridge Badagry" is a working brand name, not a checked trademark or domain.
2. The categories on browse.html are request pathways, not verified live inventory.
3. No real seller partnerships, current prices, ratings, or stock are claimed.
4. Forms use mailto: to prepare a message in the visitor's email app. They do not store or transmit form submissions to a server. Connect a secure form provider/backend before relying on them.
5. Add a business email and WhatsApp contact that you control.
6. Review the privacy notice and commission agreement for compliance with applicable Nigerian law.
7. Use only seller-authorized photos and details.
8. Test every page on a phone before publishing.

HOW TO TEST ON YOUR PHONE
- Extract the ZIP.
- Open index.html in a browser or upload the complete folder to a static host.
- Keep all HTML files, styles.css and app.js together in the same folder.
- For deployment, upload the folder contents (not just index.html) to your chosen static hosting service.
- External Google Fonts require an internet connection; system fonts remain available as fallbacks.

THIS IS A FRONT-END STARTER
It does not include a database, login system, live inventory, payments, escrow, delivery tracking, automated WhatsApp messages, seller dashboard, or an admin panel. Those require additional integration and testing.
