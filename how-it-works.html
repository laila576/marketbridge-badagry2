Simple by design

A clearer journey from need to offer.

MarketBridge is being developed as a local discovery and matching service. The first version is deliberately personal: real requests, confirmed details and direct communication.

01

Tell us what you need

Share the product or service, your budget, general location and any requirements that matter.

02

We seek suitable offers

We contact participating sellers and ask them to confirm the product, price, condition and availability.

03

Review your options

Where suitable offers are available, we share the details so you can compare and ask follow-up questions.

04

Agree on the transaction

You and the seller confirm the final price, delivery or collection arrangements, payment method and any applicable return terms.

05

Confirm the outcome

We follow up to learn whether the match worked and improve the service for future buyers.

06

Help us improve

Your feedback helps identify which categories deserve a larger catalogue and better tools.

What we're not claiming

Clear expectations matter.

This early version is not a live inventory database, payment gateway, escrow service or guarantee of product quality. Availability must be confirmed directly with the seller.

We do not publish fabricated seller reviews, stock counts or discounts. Any future verification and buyer-protection features will be described according to what is actually implemented.

The principle
✓

Know the details before you decide.

Take your time, ask questions and keep a written record of the agreed transaction.
