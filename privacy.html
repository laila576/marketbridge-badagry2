Your information

A simple privacy starting point.

This page describes the intended approach for the prototype. It must be reviewed and updated before public launch to reflect the actual data collection and applicable Nigerian privacy requirements.

Information you choose to provide

Product requests and seller interest forms may ask for contact details, product requirements, business details and location at a general area level. Provide only information needed to respond.

How information should be used

Information should be used to respond to requests, match buyers with relevant sellers, manage referrals and improve the service. Personal information should not be sold or shared with unrelated parties.

Prototype limitations

The included forms prepare a message in the user's email app; they do not submit data to a website database. If a form backend, analytics, cookies, or third-party integrations are added, this notice must be updated to accurately disclose those systems.

Contact and data requests

Before launch, add a business contact address and a process for people to ask about, correct or request deletion of information held about them. Follow applicable Nigerian data protection law and seek professional advice where necessary.
