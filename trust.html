Before you buy

Confidence begins with clear information.

We want every buyer to understand what is confirmed, what remains to be checked and who is responsible for each part of the transaction.

Practical checks for buyers

Confirm the seller and the product

Ask for current photos, exact model, condition and a clear explanation of any defects. For higher-value items, verify ownership and inspect the product before paying where practical.

Confirm the final price and delivery

Ask whether delivery, installation, accessories or other charges are included. Get the agreed terms in writing.

Use sensible payment precautions

Avoid paying an unfamiliar seller under pressure. Use a payment method with suitable protections where available, and never share passwords, PINs or one-time codes.

Keep records

Keep the listing, messages, payment evidence and delivery details in case you need to resolve a dispute.
